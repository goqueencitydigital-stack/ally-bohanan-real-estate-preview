Overview

Discover Southern Pine Lakes, Clover's latest luxury community, featuring just 37 sought-after Estate homesites located in a top-rated school district.

This stunning cul-de-sac 1 acre lot provides a peaceful and private setting while remaining conveniently close to big box retailers, delicious restaurants, and shopping. Enjoy easy access to Uptown Charlotte and CLT Airport, simplifying your daily commutes and travel plans.

With a strong sense of community and a commitment to high-quality living, Southern Pine Lakes stands out as one of South Carolina’s most desirable areas to reside. This thoughtfully designed community blends classic appeal with modern convenience, creating an ideal space for families to thrive.

You have the flexibility to choose your own builder, and all homes must adhere to a minimum size of 2,500 sq ft for single-story designs or 2,800 sq ft for two-story options. The lot has already been perked, and a complimentary house plan is available to assist you in the building process.

Come and create your dream home while enjoying the exceptional lifestyle that this unique community provides.
