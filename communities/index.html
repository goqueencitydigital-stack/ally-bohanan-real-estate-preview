Communities

Where to live in Gaston, York and Charlotte.

Eighteen towns on both sides of the state line, written like a travel guide: what each place is like on a Saturday, what to see first and what to check before you buy.

The short answer

Ally Bohanan covers Gaston County, NC (Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly, Kings Mountain, Cherryville, Dallas, Lowell and McAdenville), York County, SC (Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill) and the greater Charlotte area. Each guide below is checked against official town and tourism sources.
