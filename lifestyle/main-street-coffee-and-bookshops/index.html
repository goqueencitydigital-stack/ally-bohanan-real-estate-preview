Main Street Coffee and Bookshops

Where to sit with a cup and a new book in Mount Holly, Gastonia, Kings Mountain, Clover and York.

By Ally Bohanan

The short answer

Mount Holly has the best pairing, with Cleary’s Bookstore and Black Sheep Coffee sharing 104 S. Main St. and Owl Legs Coffee nearby. After that, try Belmont Bookshop, 57 Genova Coffee in Gastonia, The Social House by Royal T in Kings Mountain, Brick & Mortar Coffee in Clover and Books-4-Ewe in York.

Updated November 19, 2026

A good Main Street has a coffee counter and a bookshop within a few doors of each other. These towns have both. Every listing comes from current tourism or town listings; check hours before you go.

Straight answers

Before you go

When is Cleary’s Bookstore open?

Tuesday to Thursday 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., Friday and Saturday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. It is closed on Mondays. Book clubs meet Thursday evenings from 7 to 9 p.m. (Cleary’s Bookstore).

When is Brick & Mortar Coffee in Clover open?

Monday to Friday 6:30 a.m. to 8 p.m., Saturday 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Sunday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m., at 109 N. Main St. Holiday hours are shorter, and it closes on Christmas Day (Brick & Mortar Coffee).

Which of these towns are in South Carolina?

Clover and York are in York County, South Carolina. Mount Holly, Belmont, Gastonia and Kings Mountain are in Gaston County, North Carolina. Clover is a short drive from Kings Mountain and Gastonia across the state line, so you can easily visit both in one Saturday.

Are these shops independent?

Go Gaston describes Cleary’s as a woman-owned independent bookstore and Owl Legs Coffee as veteran-owned (Go Gaston). Books-4-Ewe is an independent bookstore on Congress Street in York.
