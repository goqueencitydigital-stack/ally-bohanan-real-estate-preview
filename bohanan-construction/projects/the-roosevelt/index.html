The short answer

The Roosevelt is a standalone garage-and-living build by Bohanan Construction in Gaston County, NC. It pairs about 600 square feet of living space, with a full kitchen, a bathroom and a flex space, with a garage of about 900 square feet.

Updated November 2026

The story

About The Roosevelt

Bohanan Construction introduced The Roosevelt in early 2026 as a standalone build that can serve as a primary home, an addition to an existing homesite, or a getaway on recreational property.

The team describes the vision as dark, moody and masculine. The living side holds about 600 square feet with a full kitchen, a bathroom and a flex space, next to a garage of about 900 square feet with generous ceiling height, built for vehicles, storage and shop work.

Ally and Chantzler shared the build on Instagram as it went up, from the open garage and shop shelving through the finished exterior lit at dusk.

For your own build

Who a build like The Roosevelt suits

Bohanan Construction describes The Roosevelt as a build that can be a primary home, an addition to an existing homesite, or a getaway on recreational property. The layout suits a guest suite, a multigenerational arrangement or a hobby space, with the living side and the garage side under one roof.

The build was shared on Instagram as it went up: the introduction in March 2026, the vision in May, an almost-finished update in September and a completion update in November. The photographs on this page come from those posts.

If you are thinking about a second building on land you already own, ask the planning office for your town or for Gaston County what is allowed on your lot before you design it, including any rules for a separate living space. The team can help you bring the right questions to that conversation.

Ally also helps buyers with existing homes through buyer representation at Ally Bohanan Real Estate, offered separately from Bohanan Construction.

About The Roosevelt

How big is The Roosevelt?

Bohanan Construction publishes about 600 square feet of living space, with a full kitchen, a bathroom and a flex space, next to a garage of about 900 square feet with generous ceiling height.

Can I add a Roosevelt-style build to land I already own?

Bohanan describes The Roosevelt as one option for an addition to an existing homesite. Whether a second building with living space is allowed depends on your lot and local rules, so check with your town or Gaston County first. See building on your lot.

Is there a floor plan for The Roosevelt?

Bohanan lists The Roosevelt as a plan you can customize. Floor plan drawings have not been published yet, so ask the team for current details.
