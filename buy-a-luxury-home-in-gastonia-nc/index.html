Buy · Luxury homes

Luxury, measured in details.

In a luxury purchase, the details decide the value: the craftsmanship, the setting and the privacy. Ally reads all three, and she can tell how a house was built.

Talk With a Buyer’s Agent

The short answer

Buying a luxury home in Gastonia, Belmont or the Charlotte area means judging more than finishes. Ally Bohanan, a Realtor who is also the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction, evaluates construction quality, setting and privacy, then compares the price with recent sales and with what it would cost to build. As of November 19, 2026, her Zillow profile includes sales up to $4,500,000.

Updated November 2026

Beyond the finishes

What does Ally look for in a luxury home?

  • How it was built. Framing, roofing, windows, waterproofing and mechanical systems matter more than the countertop. Ally knows where builders save money and where they should not.
  • The setting. Acreage, water frontage, views and what could be built next door. A quiet lot today can change with one rezoning.
  • Privacy. In the showing schedule, in the marketing and in how the home sits on its land.
  • Renovation potential. Older and architecturally unique homes can be the best value in the market, if the bones are right. Ally can tell you which walls move and which do not.
  • Where the value is. The asking price measured against recent sales and against replacement cost.

The difference

How is buying a luxury home different?

The buyer pool is smaller and the homes are less alike, so every part of the process needs more care. Fewer comparable sales mean appraisals can come in below contract price, and lenders may treat larger loans differently. Inspections take longer on bigger homes with more systems: pools, generators, docks, wells, outbuildings and smart-home wiring all need a qualified eye.

Some sellers prefer a quiet sale, so not every distinctive home is promoted widely. Ally watches the MLS and stays in touch with other agents. She cannot promise access to unlisted homes, but she can tell you what is likely to come up and when.

The luxury process

What are the steps to buy a luxury home?

  1. Define the life, then the house

    Setting, commute, schools, privacy and how you entertain. A clear brief keeps the search short.

  2. Prepare the financing

    Larger loans and jumbo programs can have different documentation and reserve requirements. Start the lender conversation early, or line up proof of funds for a cash purchase.

  3. Tour with a builder’s eye

    Ally flags construction quality, maintenance history and renovation potential on each showing, and helps you decide which homes deserve a full inspection.

  4. Price it against the market and the cost to build

    Ally compares recent sales and, where useful, an estimate of what it would cost to build something similar with Bohanan Construction.

  5. Negotiate and complete due diligence

    Specialist inspections, survey, appraisal, insurance and any HOA or lake rules. Ally keeps every deadline in view until closing.

Settings that draw luxury buyers

Where are luxury homes in this area?

Distinctive homes in this part of the Carolinas tend to cluster around water, historic downtowns and land. Belmont and Mount Holly sit on the Catawba River. Lake Wylie crosses the state line into York County. Gastonia has the York-Chester and Brookwood historic districts, and western Gaston County and Clover offer acreage for custom builds. Charlotte is next door.

Buy or build

When does building make more sense than buying?

Sometimes the house you want does not exist yet. If the market keeps showing you compromises, compare the cost and timeline of building on your own lot. Bohanan Construction builds luxury custom homes, and its guide to land and lots covers access, utilities and site evaluation.

Straight answers

Luxury buyer questions

What counts as a luxury home in the Gastonia area?

There is no official price line. In this market, buyers usually mean homes that stand well above the local median for their size, finish and setting: lake or river frontage, acreage, custom construction or a notable historic house. For context, 8 of the 120 sales on Ally’s Zillow profile closed at $750,000 or more as of November 19, 2026.

Why is pricing a luxury home harder?

There are fewer comparable sales, and the features that drive value, such as frontage, acreage and custom work, rarely match from one home to the next. Ally studies the closest sales, adjusts for real differences and, because she works with a builder, can estimate what it would cost to build something similar today.

Should I buy an existing luxury home or build one?

Buy if a home already matches most of your list and the price makes sense against replacement cost. Build if you need a specific layout, lot or level of finish that the market does not offer. Should I Buy or Build? and Bohanan Construction can help you decide.

Can you help me buy a luxury home before I move here?

Yes. Many of Ally’s clients relocate from out of state. She can preview homes, send honest notes on condition and setting, and help you plan an efficient visit. See the relocation guide.

Can I buy and sell a luxury home at the same time?

Yes, with careful timing. Ally can represent you on both sides and coordinate the dates so you are not forced into a rushed sale or a bridge you do not need. Talk with your lender early about financing both.
