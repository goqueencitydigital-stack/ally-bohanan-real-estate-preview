Straight answers

Questions about these videos

Are the homes in these videos still for sale?

Usually not. Most tours were filmed when a home first went on the market, and several have since sold; each description says so where we know it. Browse current listings, see homes Ally has sold, or call (704) 486-2900 to ask about a specific property.

Can I tour a home I saw in a video?

If it is still available, yes. Ally can set up a private showing. If it has sold, she can show you similar homes, lots or new builds in the same part of Gaston County or York County.

Who is Bohanan Construction?

Bohanan Construction is the custom home builder where Ally is the business manager and designer. Its videos follow builds such as The Roosevelt, The Conrad, The Amelia, The Hanson and The Azalea from bare land to finished rooms. See all projects and how the building process works.

Where are the original posts?

Every card has a link to the original post on Instagram or Facebook. The videos play here on this page so you do not need an account to watch them.
