Lifestyle · Plan My Weekend

Plan my weekend, like a local would.

Choose where you are starting, the date, how much time you have and what sounds good. You will get a realistic two- or three-stop plan built only from verified places and events, with directions and official links.

What sounds good?

How we build each plan

  • Stops come only from places and events on this site, each checked against its official source.
  • Scheduled events keep their verified start time. Places with published hours are only suggested while they are open.
  • We leave time between stops, and more when the next stop is in another town. We do not promise drive times; check your map app.
  • When there is not enough verified information for a full day, we say so and offer a shorter plan.
