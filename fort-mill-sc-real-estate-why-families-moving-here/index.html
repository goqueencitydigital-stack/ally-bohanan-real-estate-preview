Fort Mill, SC Real Estate: Why Families Are Moving Here

Why families choose Fort Mill, how its planned communities and schools shape the market, and the South Carolina tax and buying details to know first.

By Ally BohananPublished April 29, 2026Updated November 16, 20263 min read

The short answer

Families move to Fort Mill for its schools, planned communities with pools and trails, and quick I-77 access to Charlotte. Homes in the Fort Mill School District (York 4) are in steady demand, so buyers should be pre-approved, verify school assignments by address, and apply for South Carolina's 4% owner-occupant assessment ratio after closing to keep property taxes down.

Key takeaways

  1. Fort Mill School District (York School District 4) is a major reason families shop here; confirm assignments for each address.
  2. Planned communities like Baxter Village offer walkable amenities, but come with HOA dues and rules.
  3. Tega Cay is a separate city on Lake Wylie, largely served by Fort Mill schools.
  4. In South Carolina, owner-occupants must apply for the 4% legal-residence assessment ratio to avoid a higher tax bill.
  5. I-77 is the main commute route into Charlotte; test it at rush hour.

Fort Mill has grown from a small mill town into one of the most sought-after family destinations in the Charlotte region. It sits just across the South Carolina line in York County, with I-77 running straight into Charlotte. If you're considering a move, here's what draws families, what the main areas are like and what to know before you make an offer.

Why are families choosing Fort Mill?

  • Schools. Fort Mill School District, also called York School District 4, is the single biggest driver of demand. Compare individual schools on SC School Report Cards.
  • Planned communities. Many neighborhoods have pools, trails, playgrounds and community events built in.
  • Location. I-77 and the Carowinds Boulevard area put Uptown Charlotte, SouthPark and Ballantyne within a reasonable drive, and corporate offices along the corridor mean some residents work close to home.
  • Outdoors. The Anne Springs Close Greenway offers miles of trails, and Lake Wylie is minutes away.

Growth has been fast. U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts shows how much Fort Mill's population has increased, and you'll see the results in traffic and new construction.

What should families know about the schools?

Fort Mill School District serves Fort Mill and most of Tega Cay, with high schools including Fort Mill High, Nation Ford High and Catawba Ridge High. Middle schools include Springfield and Banks Trail. Because the district has grown quickly, attendance zones have been adjusted over the years.

  1. Confirm the assigned schools for every address with the district.
  2. Review each school on the SC School Report Cards.
  3. Ask about bus routes and before- and after-school care.
  4. Re-confirm before closing if schools are a deciding factor.

What's different about buying in South Carolina?

If you're moving from North Carolina or out of state, a few South Carolina rules matter:

  • Property tax assessment. An owner-occupied primary residence can be assessed at 4% of value instead of 6%, but you have to apply with the York County assessor. Missing it can mean a much higher bill.
  • Attorney closings. South Carolina closings are handled by an attorney.
  • Disclosures. Sellers complete a South Carolina Residential Property Condition Disclosure Statement.
  • Contract terms. South Carolina contracts use different forms and timelines than North Carolina's due diligence system, so read yours carefully.

How competitive is the Fort Mill market?

Well-priced homes in popular school zones can move quickly, especially in spring and early summer. To compete:

  • Get fully pre-approved before you tour. Our preferred lender can help.
  • Know the comps for the neighborhood so you can offer with confidence.
  • Read the HOA documents early so there are no surprises.
  • Be ready to move on the right home, but don't skip the inspection.

What's daily life like?

Most errands happen along SC 160, US 21 and Gold Hill Road, with plenty of restaurants, grocery stores and medical offices. Carowinds sits at the northern edge of the area. Weekends often mean the Anne Springs Close Greenway, youth sports or a day on Lake Wylie.

Frequently asked questions

What school district is Fort Mill in?

Most of Fort Mill and Tega Cay is served by Fort Mill School District, also known as York School District 4. Some nearby addresses fall into other districts, so verify each home with the district.

Are property taxes lower in Fort Mill than in North Carolina?

It depends on the home and whether you qualify for South Carolina's 4% owner-occupied assessment ratio, which must be applied for with the York County assessor. Ask your closing attorney and compare actual tax bills for homes you're considering.

Is Tega Cay part of Fort Mill?

No, Tega Cay is its own city on Lake Wylie, though it shares a ZIP code area and most of its students attend Fort Mill schools. City services and taxes differ.

How does Fort Mill compare with Lake Wylie?

Fort Mill leans toward planned suburban communities near I-77, while Lake Wylie is centered on the lake and SC 49 and 274. Our Fort Mill vs. Lake Wylie comparison covers the trade-offs.
