Living in Charlotte

What to know about Charlotte

01

Game nights

The Panthers and Charlotte FC play at Bank of America Stadium. The Hornets play at Spectrum Center, and the Knights play at Truist Field. The Lifestyle calendar tracks upcoming home dates.

02

West side outdoors

The U.S. National Whitewater Center at 5000 Whitewater Center Pkwy. covers more than 1,300 acres, with 40–50 miles of trails on the Catawba.

03

Living in the city

Camp North End and Charlotte’s older neighborhoods give the city a different pace from the towns west of the river. Ally can show you how the choices compare.
