NC First-Time Home Buyer Assistance Programs 2026

A plain-language rundown of NC Housing Finance Agency programs and federal loan options, who they're for, and how to apply through a lender.

By Ally BohananPublished May 9, 2026Updated November 16, 20262 min read

In this article
  1. What does the NC Housing Finance Agency offer?
  2. What federal loan options help first-time buyers?
  3. How do you apply?
  4. What mistakes should you avoid?
  5. What else can lower your upfront cost?
  6. Frequently asked questions

The short answer

North Carolina's main assistance comes from the NC Housing Finance Agency: the NC Home Advantage Mortgage with down payment help of up to 3% of the loan, the NC 1st Home Advantage Down Payment of $15,000 for first-time buyers and veterans, and the Community Partners Loan Pool for lower-income buyers. You apply through a participating lender.

Key takeaways

  1. NCHFA programs are accessed through participating lenders, not directly from the agency.
  2. NC Home Advantage offers down payment help of up to 3% of the loan amount for first-time and move-up buyers.
  3. NC 1st Home Advantage provides $15,000 for first-time buyers and military veterans who qualify.
  4. The Community Partners Loan Pool targets buyers below 80% of area median income.
  5. Program terms, limits and repayment rules change, so confirm current details with NCHFA and your lender.

If saving for a down payment is what's holding you back, North Carolina has real programs that can help. They come with rules on income, price, occupancy and sometimes education, and they're delivered through lenders. This guide covers the statewide programs and federal loan options. For local steps and Gaston County considerations, see our Gaston County first-time buyer guide.

What does the NC Housing Finance Agency offer?

According to the NC Housing Finance Agency, its homebuyer programs include:

ProgramAssistanceWho it's for
NC Home Advantage MortgageDown payment assistance of up to 3% of the loan amountFirst-time and move-up buyers who meet program limits
NC 1st Home Advantage Down Payment$15,000 in down payment helpFirst-time buyers (no home owned as a principal residence in the past three years) and military veterans
Community Partners Loan PoolUp to 25% of the sales price, to a maximum of $50,000Buyers with income below 80% of their county's median, through participating partners
Self-Help Loan PoolZero-interest, shared mortgage financingEligible low- and moderate-income buyers through participating organizations

Each program has its own repayment or forgiveness terms, income limits by county and household size, and price limits. Ask your lender to walk you through current terms before you rely on them.

What federal loan options help first-time buyers?

  • FHA loans allow a lower down payment and more flexible credit guidelines, with upfront and annual mortgage insurance.
  • VA loans are available to eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses, often with no down payment. Check eligibility with the VA home loan program.
  • USDA loans serve income-eligible buyers in eligible areas, often outside city centers. Check addresses on the USDA eligibility map.
  • Conventional low-down-payment loans are offered by many lenders for first-time buyers.

NCHFA assistance is typically paired with one of these first mortgages, so the right combination depends on your credit, income and the home.

How do you apply?

  1. Find a participating lender. NCHFA lists lenders on its site, or see our preferred lender page.
  2. Get pre-approved for the program, not just a standard loan. Ask the lender to confirm your income and price limits.
  3. Complete any required homebuyer education early, since it can take time to schedule.
  4. Shop within the price limit, and tell your buyer's agent you're using assistance so offers are written correctly.
  5. Allow time to close. Assistance programs can add review steps, so plan your settlement date with your lender.

What mistakes should you avoid?

  • Assuming you don't qualify without asking. "First-time buyer" can include people who haven't owned in three years.
  • Going under contract above the program's price limit.
  • Making large purchases or opening credit during the process.
  • Skipping the fine print on repayment if you sell or refinance early.
  • Comparing only rates. Look at total costs and cash to close on the Loan Estimate.

What else can lower your upfront cost?

  • Seller credits toward closing costs, within your loan's limits
  • Lender credits in exchange for a slightly higher rate
  • Employer or local assistance programs, where available

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be a first-time buyer to use NCHFA programs?

Not for all of them. NCHFA says NC Home Advantage can help first-time and move-up buyers, while NC 1st Home Advantage is for first-time buyers and veterans.

Can I combine down payment assistance with an FHA or VA loan?

Often yes, since NCHFA assistance is paired with a first mortgage. Your participating lender can confirm which loan types work with each program.

Is down payment assistance free money?

It depends on the program. Some assistance is a deferred or forgivable second loan with conditions, so ask what happens if you sell or refinance.
