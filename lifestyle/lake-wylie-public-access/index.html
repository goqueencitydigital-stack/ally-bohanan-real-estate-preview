Getting on Lake Wylie Without a Lake House

Public shoreline, a marina and a creek launch on the North Carolina side of the lake.

By Ally Bohanan
Reviewed November 19, 2026

Stops

Towns

The short answer

On the Gaston County side, there are three easy ways onto Lake Wylie without owning a dock. South Point Access in Belmont has public shoreline for $7 per vehicle. Tailrace Marina in Mount Holly has boating and paddling access, and River Street Park in Mount Holly has a canoe and kayak launch onto Dutchman’s Creek.

Updated November 19, 2026

You do not need a dock to spend a day on Lake Wylie. The lake covers about 13,443 acres with roughly 325 miles of shoreline (SC DNR), and the Gaston County side has three easy ways in.

If you live here

Lakefront, lake-view and lake-access are three different things, and the difference shows up in price, insurance and how often you actually get on the water. The public access points above are what lake life looks like for many people on the Gaston County side. Straight answers

Before you go

What does South Point Access cost, and when is it open?

$7 per vehicle and $20 per bus. It is open 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. from April 1 through October 31, and 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. from November 1 through March 31 (Gaston County).

Can I swim at South Point?

Gaston County opens the swim beach from Memorial Day through Labor Day only. Pets are not allowed in the beach area, and dogs elsewhere in the park must be on a leash six feet or shorter (Gaston County).

Where do I park at Tailrace Marina?

Tailrace Marina, 1010 Marina Village Drive, Mount Holly, says it has ample parking shared with JR Cash Grill & Bar (Tailrace Marina).

Is Lake Wylie in North Carolina or South Carolina?

Both. SC DNR describes Lake Wylie as being on the border between the two states, between Rock Hill and Charlotte. About 7,316 of its acres are in South Carolina, and the lake is owned and managed by Duke Energy (SC DNR).
