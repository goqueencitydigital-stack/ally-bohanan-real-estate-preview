The short answer

Two York County, SC communities just south of Charlotte. Fort Mill pairs a historic Main Street with planned communities and a 2,100-acre greenway; Lake Wylie is an unincorporated lake community. Both are covered by Ally Bohanan, who is licensed in North Carolina and South Carolina.

Question by question

How they compare

Housing and lots

Fort Mill

Planned communities such as Baxter Village and Kingsley alongside older homes near Main Street.

Lake Wylie

Lakefront, lake-view and lake-access neighborhoods, plus homes inland from the water.

Downtown

Fort Mill

A small historic Main Street with the Fort Mill History Museum.

Lake Wylie

No traditional downtown. Lake Wylie is an unincorporated community.

Public outdoor access

Fort Mill

Anne Springs Close Greenway: 2,100 acres and 40+ miles of trails, by day pass or membership.

Lake Wylie

The lake itself: about 13,443 acres and roughly 325 miles of shoreline, managed by Duke Energy.

Destinations

Fort Mill

The SC Strawberry Festival and Christmas Village at Walter Y. Elisha Park.

Lake Wylie

Boating and the shoreline; Tega Cay sits on a nearby 1,600-acre peninsula.

First visit

Fort Mill

Main Street and the History Museum, then the Greenway.

Lake Wylie

Drive the shore and see how lakefront and lake-access neighborhoods differ.
