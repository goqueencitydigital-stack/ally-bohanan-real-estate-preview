Buy · Relocation

Moving to the Carolinas, from anywhere.

Many of Ally’s clients arrive from out of state. Before you ever tour a house, she helps you understand the towns on both sides of the state line.

Talk With a Buyer’s Agent

The short answer

Relocating to the Charlotte area usually starts with one question: which side of the state line? Gaston County, North Carolina sits west of Charlotte, and York County, South Carolina sits to the south. Ally Bohanan, licensed in both states and based in Belmont, helps you compare towns, taxes and commutes, plan an efficient visit and buy with a builder’s eye on condition.

Updated November 2026

Your relocation plan

How do you plan a move to the Charlotte area?

Six steps, in the order that saves the most time and the fewest surprises.

  1. Start with how you want to live

    Use Find My Carolina Community to narrow the towns, then read the guides. The comparisons put neighboring towns side by side.

  2. Understand the state line

    Gaston County is in North Carolina. York County, including Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill, is in South Carolina. Ally is licensed in both states. Taxes, schools and closing customs differ, so ask early.

  3. Get pre-approved before you fly in

    Ally works with a preferred local lender, and you can choose any lender you like. A pre-approval means your offer can move when the right house appears.

  4. Plan your visit around a Saturday

    Try Plan My Weekend to build a day around the towns on your list, and drive your future commute at rush hour.

  5. Tour with someone who notices

    Honest notes and a builder’s eye on condition, especially if you are deciding before you can walk every house yourself.

  6. Close from wherever you are

    North Carolina closings are usually handled by a closing attorney. Ask the closing office early about remote signing options and wiring instructions.

Two states, one metro

North Carolina or South Carolina: what changes?

  • Property taxes. Gaston County charges 59.9 cents per $100 of assessed value as of July 1, 2026, plus a city rate inside town limits. South Carolina taxes an owner-occupied primary residence at a 4 percent assessment ratio instead of 6 percent, once you apply with the county assessor.
  • Schools. Gaston County Schools covers the North Carolina side. York County has separate districts for Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill.
  • Contracts and closing. Each state uses its own purchase forms and customs. In North Carolina, buyers pay a negotiated due diligence fee directly to the seller.
  • Lake Wylie. The lake crosses the state line, so two homes on the same water can be in different states. The Lake Wylie guide explains how to tell.

Ask a tax adviser how income tax and vehicle taxes would affect your household before you decide.

Common surprises

What do relocating buyers often miss?

  • Commute times at rush hour, especially on the I-85 and I-485 crossings of the Catawba River.
  • Flood-zone status near the river and its creeks. Check the NC Flood Risk Information System.
  • Well and septic on rural property, and the inspections each one needs.
  • HOA rules in planned communities, from fences to boats.
  • Whether building would serve them better than buying. See Should I Buy or Build?

Straight answers

Relocation questions

Is Gaston County in North Carolina or South Carolina?

North Carolina. Gaston County sits just west of Charlotte. York County, which includes Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill, is directly south across the South Carolina line. Many people live in one state and work in the other.

How do property taxes differ between North Carolina and South Carolina here?

In Gaston County, the county rate is 59.9 cents per $100 of assessed value as of July 1, 2026, plus any city rate. South Carolina assesses an owner-occupied primary residence at 4 percent of value instead of 6 percent, but you must apply with the county. State income taxes also differ, so ask a tax adviser how a move would affect you.

Can I buy a home before I visit in person?

Some buyers do, but Ally recommends at least one visit to the towns on your list. She can preview homes, share honest notes and help you plan a tight trip. The due diligence period lets you inspect thoroughly before you are fully committed.

How far are these towns from Charlotte Douglas International Airport?

Belmont and Mount Holly sit just across the Catawba River from the airport side of Charlotte, which is one reason frequent travelers look there. Drive times depend on traffic, so test the route at the hours you would actually travel.

Do schools change at the state line?

Yes. North Carolina towns are served by Gaston County Schools, and York County, South Carolina has several separate districts, including Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill. Confirm the assignment for the exact address with the district.
