The story

About The Brick Arches Home

This finished Bohanan home is the one featured in Bohanan Construction’s property film. Out front, three brick arches frame the entry porch under a steep central gable, with gas-style lanterns and landscaped beds along the foundation.

Inside, an arched window wall opens the great room, the kitchen centers on a professional-style range under a plaster hood, and a coffered ceiling, built-in buffet and arched French doors carry the detail from room to room.

Upstairs, a bonus room has its own wet bar and wine refrigerator. Out back, a covered brick porch with a stained wood ceiling looks over a wide lawn.
