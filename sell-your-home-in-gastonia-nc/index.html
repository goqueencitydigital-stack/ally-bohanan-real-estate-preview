Seller preparation guide

How to prepare your home to sell

Most of what makes a listing succeed happens before it goes live. This is the order Ally recommends. The timeline tool above turns it into a week-by-week plan.

1. Start with a pricing conversation, not a number

Ally walks through the home, studies recent nearby sales and current competition, and explains the range she would list in and why. Online estimates can be a starting point, but they cannot see your kitchen, your lot or the street. That is why Ally prices homes in person.

2. Gather your documents early

Mortgage payoff, HOA contacts, warranties, permits, surveys and utility averages. Buyers ask for them, and having them ready keeps the contract moving.

3. Repair what buyers will notice

Ally’s construction background helps her separate the repairs that pay for themselves from the ones buyers will overlook. A pre-listing inspection can surface surprises while you still have time to choose.

4. Declutter, then stage

Pack what you will not need for a few months. Then stage the rooms that matter most: the living room, kitchen and primary bedroom. See home staging.

5. Prepare for photography

Deep clean, open the blinds, turn every light on, clear the counters and move the cars. Photography day sets the first impression for most buyers.

6. Plan for showings

Agree on notice times, a pet plan and lockbox access before launch, so the first weekend goes smoothly.
