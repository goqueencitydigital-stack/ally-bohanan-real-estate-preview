Cabarrus County, North Carolina

Concord, on race weekend and every other

Concord sits northeast of Charlotte in Cabarrus County. It is best known for Charlotte Motor Speedway and zMAX Dragway, and its historic downtown is quieter than that reputation suggests.

Where is Concord, and how does it relate to Charlotte?

Concord is the largest city in Cabarrus County and its county seat, northeast of Charlotte along I-85. Most people who move here think of it as its own city rather than a suburb, with a courthouse downtown, its own airport and a long list of employers. From Gaston County, the drive runs the full width of Charlotte, so buyers comparing Concord with Belmont or Gastonia are really choosing which side of the metro to build a life around.

What is downtown Concord like?

Concord was founded in 1796 after a dispute over where the new county seat should go, and the town took a name meaning harmony. Union Street became the main road and still is. The 1876 Historic Cabarrus County Courthouse anchors downtown and holds the Davis Theatre and gallery space, with locally owned restaurants and shops along the surrounding blocks.

Which Concord neighborhoods have historic homes?

The City of Concord protects three historic districts. The North and South Union Street Historic Districts were established in 1982 and are listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Edgewood was designated as a local historic district in 1988. Exterior changes in these districts go before the Historic Preservation Commission, which matters if you plan to renovate.

What is western Concord known for?

The west side is where Concord gets its racing reputation. Charlotte Motor Speedway, zMAX Dragway and Concord Mills sit here, along with Concord-Padgett Regional Airport, which the city owns. That part of town is busier and more commercial, and newer subdivisions spread out from the I-85 corridor. Event schedules at the speedway affect traffic, so check the calendar before you tour on a race weekend.

How do Cabarrus County property taxes compare with Gaston County?

The North Carolina Department of Revenue lists the Cabarrus County rate at 57.6 cents per $100 of assessed value for 2025–26, after a 2024 reappraisal. Gaston County’s rate is 59.9 cents as of July 1, 2026. City taxes are added on top for homes inside Concord city limits, so ask for the full combined rate on any address you are serious about.

Concord or Gaston County: how should you decide?

Start with where you will drive most. If work, family or school sits northeast of Charlotte, Concord saves real time. If your week points toward the airport, the west side of Charlotte or York County in South Carolina, Gaston County towns like Belmont, Mount Holly and Gastonia usually fit better.
