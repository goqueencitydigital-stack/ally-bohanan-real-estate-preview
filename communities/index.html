The short answer

Gaston County, NC and York County, SC meet at the state line west and south of Charlotte. Ally Bohanan covers both: Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly, Kings Mountain, Cherryville, Dallas, Lowell and McAdenville in Gaston County; Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill in York County; and Charlotte, Lincolnton, Concord, Mooresville and Wesley Chapel around the metro. Start with the county, because it sets your tax system and school district, then use the guides below to compare towns. Each one is checked against official town, county and tourism sources.

Straight answers

Community questions

What is the difference between Gaston County, NC and York County, SC?

They sit on opposite sides of the state line. Gaston County towns are in North Carolina, west of Charlotte along I-85 and the Catawba River. York County towns are in South Carolina, south of Charlotte around Lake Wylie and the I-77 corridor. The line changes your property tax rules, your school district and which state’s real estate license your agent needs. Ally is licensed in both states. See how to compare towns across the line.

Which towns are on Lake Wylie?

On the South Carolina side, the unincorporated Lake Wylie community in York County is built around the lake, and Tega Cay sits on a peninsula nearby. On the North Carolina side, Belmont reaches the water at South Point, where Duke Energy runs the South Point Access Area. Read the Lake Wylie guide for the difference between lakefront, lake-view and lake-access homes.

Which school district serves each town?

In Gaston County, every town in our guides is part of one countywide district, Gaston County Schools; Kings Mountain also reaches into Cleveland County, so confirm which county a Kings Mountain home is in first. York County has four school districts: Clover School District serves Clover and the Lake Wylie area, Fort Mill School District serves Fort Mill and Tega Cay, Rock Hill Schools serves most of Rock Hill, and York School District 1 serves York and western York County. District lines do not match mailing addresses, so check the exact home on York County’s school locator or the Gaston County Schools address search.

Which towns are closest to Charlotte?

In Gaston County, Belmont and Mount Holly face Charlotte across the Catawba River, and I-85 and US 29/74 carry traffic east from Gastonia, Lowell and McAdenville toward the city. In York County, the Town of Fort Mill places itself about 19 miles south of Charlotte on I-77, and the City of Rock Hill about 20 miles. We do not publish drive times; traffic varies too much. Ally can help you test the drive at the hours you would make it.

How do property taxes differ between NC and SC?

North Carolina property tax is set and collected by each county; the NC Department of Revenue lists Gaston County at 59.9 cents per $100 of assessed value for 2026–27, with property valued at 100% of its appraised value and reappraised at least every eight years. Towns add their own rate inside town limits. South Carolina assesses a primary residence at 4% of value once the owner applies, and second homes, other residential property and land at 6%, according to the SC Department of Revenue; York County takes the application through its Assessor’s Office. Ask for the combined estimate on any specific address.
