About

Ally Bohanan, Realtor and builder.

Ally is a broker-in-charge licensed in North Carolina and South Carolina, with an office on Main Street in Belmont. She is also the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction.

The short answer

Ally Bohanan is a Realtor and broker-in-charge licensed in North Carolina and South Carolina, brokered by Northstar Real Estate, LLC, with an office at 26 North Main Street in Belmont. She helps buyers, sellers, investors and custom home clients across Gaston County, York County and the Charlotte area, and she is the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction. As of November 19, 2026, Zillow records 120 sales on her profile.

Updated November 2026

Why do clients choose Ally?

Ally pairs deep local knowledge with careful attention to detail. Her aim is simple: help every client make confident, informed decisions that support their long-term financial and lifestyle goals.

Her construction background gives clients a different kind of advantage. Ally can judge a home’s build quality, its renovation potential and its long-term value, not just its finishes. Clients notice. Jennifer Grant wrote that what sets Ally apart is her “knowledge of renovations, costs, timelines, and construction.”

What does Ally help with?

  • Home buying and home selling
  • Luxury home representation
  • Custom home guidance with Bohanan Construction
  • Investment property consulting
  • Pricing consultations and pricing strategy
  • Contract negotiation and transaction management
  • Relocation help
  • Notary and consultation services

Where does Ally work?

Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly, Kings Mountain and the rest of Gaston County; Lake Wylie and Fort Mill in South Carolina; and Charlotte, Concord and the surrounding communities in Mecklenburg County. See every community guide.

Contact Ally at (704) 486-2900 or ally@amb-homes.com. Office: 26 North Main Street, Suite 2, Belmont, NC 28012. NC License #315170. Brokered by Northstar Real Estate, LLC.

Straight answers

About Ally

Is Ally Bohanan licensed in North Carolina and South Carolina?

Yes. Ally is a broker-in-charge licensed in both states. Her North Carolina broker license is #315170, and she is associated with Northstar Real Estate, LLC. You can confirm any North Carolina license on the NC Real Estate Commission website.

What brokerage is Ally Bohanan with?

Ally Bohanan Real Estate is brokered by Northstar Real Estate, LLC. The office is at 26 North Main Street, Suite 2, in downtown Belmont.

Does Ally build homes?

Ally is the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction, which builds custom homes in the area. That is why her clients get a builder’s view of condition, renovation potential and cost when they buy or sell.

Which areas does Ally serve?

Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly, Kings Mountain and the rest of Gaston County; Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill in South Carolina; and Charlotte and the surrounding metro, including Concord. See the community guides.

How many homes has Ally sold?

As of November 19, 2026, her Zillow profile records 120 sales since 2019, 35 of them in the past 12 months. The full list is on the homes sold page.
