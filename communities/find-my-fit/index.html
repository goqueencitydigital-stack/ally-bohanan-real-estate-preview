    How the matching works

    Every town in our guides is tagged with the kinds of Saturdays, surroundings and places it is closest to, based on the sources listed on its guide. Each answer adds points:

    • Saturday: 3 points if the town is known for that kind of day.
    • Surroundings: 2 points if the town offers that setting.
    • Land: “An acre or more” or “land to build on” adds 2 points to towns with room to spread out; “a small, easy yard” adds 1 point to towns with a downtown.
    • Places you reach: 1 point for each place on that side of the region.

    We show up to three towns. Ties go to the town with more matches. We never use demographics, school rankings, safety scores or commute estimates. If two towns tie closely, both are reasonable, and Ally can help you choose.
