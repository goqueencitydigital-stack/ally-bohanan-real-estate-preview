Moving From Charlotte to Gastonia: Pros, Cons and Cost Comparison (2026)

An honest look at what you gain and give up moving from Charlotte to Gastonia, including housing, taxes, commute, amenities and how to compare real costs.

By Ally BohananPublished May 5, 20262 min read

In this article

The short answer

Moving from Charlotte to Gastonia usually trades a shorter commute and more city amenities for lower housing costs, more space for your budget and a quieter pace. Gastonia sits west of Charlotte along I-85. Commute times vary a lot with traffic, so compare real listings, property tax bills and your rush-hour drive before you decide.

Key takeaways

  1. Housing is usually the biggest cost difference. Compare actual listings in the areas you'd consider, not citywide averages.
  2. Property taxes depend on the county and city rates, so calculate them for each specific home.
  3. The I-85 commute is the biggest trade-off, so test it at rush hour.
  4. Gastonia is mostly car-dependent, with less nightlife and fewer dining options than Charlotte, though it has growing downtown amenities.
  5. Remote and hybrid workers often get the most out of the move.

Many people who work in or around Charlotte look west to Gastonia for more space for their budget and a quieter pace. The move makes a lot of sense for some households and less for others. Here's how to weigh it honestly.

How do you compare costs accurately?

Citywide averages don't tell you much. Compare the homes you'd actually buy:

  1. Pull listings and recent sales in two or three neighborhoods in each area.
  2. Estimate property taxes with the current county and city rates from Gaston County and Mecklenburg County. North Carolina also taxes vehicles annually.
  3. Get homeowners insurance quotes for each home.
  4. Add HOA dues and utility costs, including whether the home has city water or a well.
  5. Price out your commute, including gas, tolls if you'll use them, extra wear on your car and your time.

Put the totals into our mortgage calculator to compare monthly payments side by side.

What are the pros?

  • More space for your budget: Larger lots and yards are often easier to find.
  • A quieter pace: Less density and congestion in daily life
  • Charlotte is still within reach: Jobs, the airport, concerts and games are a drive away.
  • Room to grow: Some buyers find newer construction or the option to build. Ally also runs Bohanan Construction, which builds custom homes in Gastonia.

What are the cons?

  • Commute: Traffic on I-85 can stretch a short drive into a long one.
  • Car dependency: Expect to drive for most errands.
  • Fewer big-city amenities: There's less nightlife and fewer dining options.
  • Neighborhoods vary: Visit at different times of day before you decide.

Tip: Before you commit, drive from your target neighborhood to work on a weekday morning and home again in the evening rush. It's the most useful test you can do.

How do you decide?

Ask yourself these questions:

  • How many days a week will you drive into Charlotte?
  • Would you rather have more space and a yard, or a shorter drive?
  • Which amenities do you use every week, and where are they?
  • Does the monthly saving still hold up once you add commuting costs?

If Gastonia still looks good, compare it with nearby towns on our communities page. Belmont and Mount Holly are closer to Charlotte, while Dallas and Kings Mountain have more of a small-town feel.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

How long is the commute from Gastonia to Charlotte?

It depends heavily on where you start, where you're going and the traffic on I-85. A drive that's quick off-peak can take much longer at rush hour, so test your actual route before you commit.

Is Gastonia cheaper than Charlotte?

For housing, buyers can often get more home or land for the money, but it varies by neighborhood. Compare specific listings, tax bills, insurance quotes and utility costs.

What are the downsides of moving to Gastonia?

The commute, limited public transit and fewer big-city amenities. Neighborhoods also vary a lot, so take time to visit the areas you're considering.

Will I still be close to the airport?

Gastonia is on the west side of the metro, and I-85 runs toward Charlotte Douglas International Airport, which helps for frequent travelers. Check the drive time from the specific neighborhood.
