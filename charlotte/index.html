Charlotte, the city next door

Many of Ally’s clients live in Gaston or York County and treat Charlotte as the big city next door, for game nights, concerts and the airport. Others want to live in the city itself. This guide covers both.

Charlotte

The short answer

Many of Ally’s clients live in Gaston or York County and treat Charlotte as the big city next door, for game nights, concerts and the airport. The city next door: stadiums, the whitewater center and Camp North End. Ally Bohanan has 4 recorded sales in Charlotte (Zillow, November 19, 2026).

Updated November 2026

Key facts

  • NFL & MLSBank of America Stadium
  • NBASpectrum Center, 333 E. Trade St.
  • BaseballTruist Field, 324 S. Mint St.
  • OutdoorsU.S. National Whitewater Center, 1,300+ acres

Game nights

The Panthers and Charlotte FC play at Bank of America Stadium. The Hornets play at Spectrum Center, and the Knights play at Truist Field. The Lifestyle calendar tracks upcoming home dates.

West side outdoors

The U.S. National Whitewater Center at 5000 Whitewater Center Pkwy. covers more than 1,300 acres, with 40–50 miles of trails on the Catawba.

Living in the city

Camp North End and Charlotte’s older neighborhoods give the city a different pace from the towns west of the river. Ally can show you how the choices compare.
