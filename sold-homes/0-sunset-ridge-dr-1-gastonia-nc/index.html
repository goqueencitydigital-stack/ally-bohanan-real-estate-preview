Before you buy land here

A lot can look ready to build and still carry surprises. These checks come before any offer or building plan. Ally’s guide to buying land and building and Should I Buy or Build? walk through the rest, and Bohanan Construction can talk through a build.

  1. Pull the parcel recordStart with Gaston County GIS property search: parcel lines, acreage and ownership. A licensed surveyor confirms the boundaries on the ground.
  2. Confirm zoning and what you can buildAsk Gaston County Planning and Zoning what the zoning allows and whether a town’s rules apply instead. Check recorded restrictions or covenants, too.
  3. Plan water and septic earlyWithout public sewer, a home needs a septic permit, and a private well needs its own permit. See Gaston County Environmental Health: on-site wastewater and wells.
  4. Check flood zones, access and utilitiesConfirm road access, power and any floodplain on the lot before you plan a house site. For Gaston County, see the flood zone checklist.
