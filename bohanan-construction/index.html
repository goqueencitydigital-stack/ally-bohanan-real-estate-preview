The process

Five steps to your front door

Building a custom home involves hundreds of decisions. The process organizes them so you always know where you are and what comes next.

The full building process

  1. Dream and discover

    An unhurried first conversation with Chantzler and Ally about who the home is for, what matters most, and your timing. You also get an honest look at budget ranges and next steps.

  2. Land and location

    Whether you own land, are under contract or are still looking, the team helps you understand what a homesite needs before the design is final: access, slope, clearing, drainage, utilities, HOA guidelines, setbacks and permitting.

  3. Design and selections

    Choose a plan to customize or bring your own, then shape the exterior and interior with Ally’s design guidance: exterior materials, roofline, windows and doors, flooring, cabinetry, countertops, tile and lighting.

  4. Pricing and planning

    Your answers become a personalized proposal built from your land, your plan and your selections, with scope, allowances and assumptions spelled out. A construction agreement follows when you are ready.

  5. Build and welcome home

    Permitting and site preparation, regular progress updates, selections installed as planned, and a final walkthrough before move-in. Chantzler oversees construction on site the whole way.

Straight answers

Building with Bohanan Construction

What does Bohanan Construction build?

Custom homes, new construction, and remodels, renovations and repairs. Repairs range from wood rot to storm damage. From time to time the team also builds homes that are offered for sale, such as The Conrad.

Do I need to own land before I contact Bohanan Construction?

No. Some clients already own land, some are under contract, and others are still looking. Ally is also a licensed real estate broker with Ally Bohanan Real Estate, so she can help you look at a property before you commit. See finding land and building on your lot.

Who will I work with?

You work directly with the owners. Chantzler leads the company and oversees every build in the field, from site work and framing through the final details. Ally manages the business side and guides clients through design and selections.

Where does Bohanan Construction build?

The office is at 258 West Main Avenue, Unit #262 C, in downtown Gastonia, NC 28052, and office visits are by appointment. The team builds in Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly, Kings Mountain, Dallas and surrounding Gaston County communities. Ask about your location.

How long does a custom home take to build?

It depends on the design, permitting, site work, selections and weather. The team discusses realistic expectations for your specific project instead of quoting a one-size-fits-all schedule.

Can I bring my own floor plan?

Yes. You can start from a Bohanan plan and customize it, bring plans from your architect or designer, or start from a blank page. Bohanan has built alongside design partners such as Plan.it Design.
