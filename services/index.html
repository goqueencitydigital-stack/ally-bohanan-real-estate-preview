  • Moving from out of state? The relocation guide explains the state line, taxes and schools.

The difference

What makes Ally’s approach different?

Two things. First, Ally prices homes by hand, in person, and explains the reasoning. There is no automated valuation. Second, she is also the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction, so she reads a house the way a builder does: condition, renovation potential and what it would cost to build today.

As of November 19, 2026, Zillow shows 120 sales on her profile. See the homes she has sold and what clients say.

The process

How does working with Ally work?

  1. A first conversation

    By phone or in person, at no cost. Ally asks about your timing, your goals and what you already know, and you review the state’s Working With Real Estate Agents disclosure.

  2. A plan built around your move

    For sellers, a pricing consultation and a week-by-week preparation plan. For buyers, a lender introduction, a shortlist of towns and a search set up around how you want to live.

  3. A written agreement

    Before Ally markets a home or makes an offer for you, North Carolina requires a written agreement that spells out her duties and compensation. She reviews it with you line by line.

  4. Representation through closing

    Showings, negotiation, due diligence, appraisal and the closing attorney, coordinated by Ally and her team until the keys change hands.

Straight answers

Service questions

What real estate services does Ally Bohanan offer?

Pricing consultations, listing representation, home staging guidance and luxury marketing for sellers; buyer representation, luxury buying and relocation help for buyers; custom home guidance with Bohanan Construction; and notary and consultation services.

Where should I start if I need to buy and sell?

Start with a lender conversation and a pricing consultation. Knowing what your home should sell for and what you can borrow tells you which order makes sense.

Does Ally work in South Carolina?

Yes. Ally is licensed in North Carolina and South Carolina and works across York County, including Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, York and Rock Hill.

Can Ally help if I want to build instead of buy?

Yes. Ally is the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction, which builds custom homes. She can help you compare an existing home with a build, find land and plan the timing. See land and lots.

Do I need a different agent for South Carolina?

Not with Ally. She is licensed in North Carolina and South Carolina, so a move between Gaston County and towns such as Fort Mill, Rock Hill or Lake Wylie can stay with one agent. Taxes, disclosures and contract forms differ by state, and she walks you through each set.

Does Ally help investors?

Yes. Ally offers investment property consulting, from evaluating a rental’s condition and likely repairs to weighing whether to sell or hold. A one-on-one consultation is a good place to start.

Is there a cost to talk with Ally?

No. A first conversation and a pricing consultation are free and carry no obligation. Any agreement and compensation are explained in writing before you sign.
