Sell · Home staging

Home Staging in Gastonia, NC Staged to be remembered.

Staging helps buyers picture themselves in a home, both online and in person. Ally’s team plans the rooms that matter, from a full furniture plan to simple edits of what you already own.

Request a Pricing Consultation

The short answer

Home staging prepares a house so buyers can picture living in it, first in listing photos and then in person. For Gastonia-area sellers, Ally Bohanan and her team plan staging room by room as part of listing preparation: furniture and décor placement, decluttering and depersonalizing, lighting, paint and accessories, and curb appeal. Ally confirms the scope and any cost for your home before work begins.

Updated November 2026

What’s included

What does home staging include?

  • Furniture and décor placement. Arranging rooms for flow, scale and the angles the photographer will use.
  • Decluttering and depersonalizing. Clearing surfaces and packing personal items so buyers see the house, not the household.
  • Lighting, paint and accessories. Bright bulbs, neutral touch-ups where needed, and a few well-chosen pieces that add warmth.
  • Curb appeal. The front door, walkway, beds and porch, because that is the first photo and the first impression.

Ally and her team actively work to get your property show-ready, and her design work at Bohanan Construction shapes how she thinks about space, light and finishes.

Staging, in order

How does staging fit into selling your home?

  1. Walk-through and pricing

    Staging starts with the pricing consultation, where Ally notes what each room needs.

  2. Repairs first

    Fix what an inspector would flag before spending effort on décor.

  3. Declutter and pack

    Pack what you will not need for a few months. It makes the move easier too.

  4. Stage the rooms that matter

    Living room, kitchen, primary bedroom and the entry, then the rest.

  5. Clean and photograph

    A deep clean, every light on, blinds open, counters clear and cars out of the drive.

  6. Keep it show-ready

    A short daily reset list keeps the home ready for showings while you live there.

Priorities

Which rooms should you stage first?

Put your effort where buyers look longest. The living room sets the tone of the listing. The kitchen gets the most scrutiny. The primary bedroom should feel calm and uncluttered. After those, give every spare room a clear purpose. A room with a treadmill, a desk and a crib reads as storage.

Outside, trim the beds, sweep the walk, and make the front door look cared for. For homes on acreage or water, the view and the approach matter as much as any room.

Two situations

Staging an occupied home or a vacant one?

Occupied homes are usually staged by editing: fewer pieces, clear counters, open sightlines and personal items packed away. It costs little and works well.

Vacant homes can feel smaller and colder in photos than they are. Furniture in the main rooms helps buyers understand scale. Ally will tell you whether your home needs it and confirm the scope before anything is arranged.

Straight answers

Staging questions

Is home staging worth it?

For most homes, some level of staging is. Buyers decide quickly from listing photos whether to tour, and rooms that are clear, bright and arranged for how people live photograph better. The right amount depends on your home, your price and whether you are still living there.

Can a home be staged while I still live in it?

Yes. Occupied staging usually means editing what you own: removing extra furniture, clearing surfaces, packing personal items and rearranging for flow and light. Ally will give you a room-by-room list you can work through before photography.

Which rooms matter most?

The living room, kitchen and primary bedroom carry the most weight in photos and showings. The front entry and curb appeal set the first impression. Spare rooms should read clearly as an office, guest room or bedroom.

What does staging cost?

It depends on the home and the scope, from a consultation and a to-do list to furniture brought in for a vacant home. Ally will confirm the scope and any cost for your home in writing before work begins.

Should I make repairs or stage first?

Repairs first, then declutter, then stage, then clean and photograph. The selling timeline puts those tasks in order for your list date.
