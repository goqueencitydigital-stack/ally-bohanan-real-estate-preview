Meet Ally

A Realtor who knows how houses are built

Ally Bohanan is a North Carolina and South Carolina real estate broker working from an office on Main Street in Belmont. She is also the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction, the custom-home company she runs with Chantzler Bohanan.

When you walk a house with Ally, she notices the details. Is that wall original? What would moving that kitchen involve? What should the inspection look at? Clients say it is the difference they feel most.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is Ally Bohanan’s office?

Ally works from 26 North Main Street, Suite 2, in downtown Belmont, North Carolina. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Saturday, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Which areas does Ally serve?

Gaston County, York County and the greater Charlotte area, including Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly, Dallas, Lake Wylie, Clover, Fort Mill, Rock Hill and Charlotte itself.

Is Ally licensed in both North and South Carolina?

Yes. Ally Bohanan is a North Carolina broker (license 315170) and a South Carolina associate (license 114167), brokered by Northstar Real Estate, LLC.

How does a pricing consultation work?

Ally walks through your home in person, studies recent nearby sales and talks through your timing. You get a pricing and preparation plan from a person, not an automated estimate.

Can Ally help me build a custom home?

Yes. Ally also manages Bohanan Construction with builder Chantzler Bohanan. If you already own land, start with the building process. If you need land, start with the land and lots guide.

How do I reach Ally?

Call or text (704) 486-2900, email ally@amb-homes.com, or send the short form on this page. Ally answers personally.
