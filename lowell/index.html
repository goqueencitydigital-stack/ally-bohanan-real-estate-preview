Gaston County, North Carolina

Lowell, on the South Fork

Lowell grew up around Woodlawn Mill, built in 1848, and was incorporated in 1879. Today it is a small town between Gastonia and Belmont with the South Fork River, Spencer Mountain and about 12 miles of trails at George Poston Park.
