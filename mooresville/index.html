Why is Mooresville called Race City USA?

The town says it plainly: Mooresville is home to many NASCAR teams and drivers, an IndyCar team and racing technology suppliers. It is also home to the corporate headquarters of Lowe’s and Universal Technical Institute’s NASCAR Technical Institute. Those employers shape the commute patterns and explain why so many households here have ties to racing or retail.

How big is Lake Norman?

Lake Norman was created when Duke Energy’s predecessor built Cowans Ford Dam on the Catawba River, starting in 1959. North Carolina’s Department of Natural and Cultural Resources describes it as more than 32,000 acres, the largest manmade body of water in the state, touching Catawba, Iredell, Lincoln and Mecklenburg counties. Mooresville sits on its northeastern shore.

Which schools serve a Mooresville address?

This is the question that trips up the most buyers. Mooresville has its own public school system, the Mooresville Graded School District, alongside Iredell-Statesville Schools. A Mooresville mailing address does not guarantee either one. Both districts publish zoning maps, and you should confirm the assignment for the exact property before you make an offer.

What is downtown Mooresville like?

Downtown is compact and walkable, with the Mooresville Museum, Mooresville Arts in the old train depot and D.E. Turner Hardware, which has been on Main Street since 1899.

How do Iredell County taxes compare with Gaston County?

The North Carolina Department of Revenue lists Iredell County at 50 cents per $100 of assessed value for 2025–26. Gaston County is 59.9 cents as of July 1, 2026. Homes inside Mooresville town limits also pay a town rate, and lakefront values push total bills higher, so compare the full estimate rather than the county rate alone.

Lake Norman or Lake Wylie?

Both lakes sit on the Catawba River chain. Lake Norman is larger and farther north. Lake Wylie is closer to Belmont, Gastonia and York County, South Carolina, and it is where Ally works most often. If your work or family points west or south of Charlotte, tour Lake Wylie before you decide. Lincoln County’s side of Lake Norman is another option closer to Gaston County.
