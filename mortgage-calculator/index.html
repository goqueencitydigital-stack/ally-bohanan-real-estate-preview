The short answer

This calculator estimates a monthly mortgage payment for a home in Gastonia or nearby: principal and interest from your price, down payment, rate and term, plus property tax and insurance if you enter them. It is not a loan offer, and it leaves out mortgage insurance, HOA dues and closing costs. Confirm real numbers with a lender.

How it works

How is a mortgage payment calculated?

Most home loans are amortized. Each monthly payment is the same, but early payments go mostly to interest and later payments go mostly to principal. The calculator uses the standard amortization formula with the price minus your down payment as the loan amount.

Your full monthly housing cost is often called PITI: principal, interest, taxes and insurance. Add mortgage insurance and HOA dues where they apply. The Loan Estimate a lender gives you lists each of these.

The fine print

What does this calculator leave out?

  • Mortgage insurance. Usually required on conventional loans with less than 20 percent down, and on FHA loans.
  • HOA dues. Common in planned communities around Belmont, Fort Mill and Lake Wylie.
  • Flood insurance. Required by lenders in mapped flood zones near the Catawba River and its creeks.
  • Closing costs. Lender fees, attorney fees, title insurance and prepaid items, paid at closing.
  • Due diligence fee and earnest money. In North Carolina, both are paid before closing and credited to you if the sale closes.

Local taxes

What are property taxes like around Gastonia?

In North Carolina, the county and the city each set a rate per $100 of assessed value. Gaston County’s rate is 59.9 cents as of July 1, 2026. Homes inside a city such as Gastonia, Belmont or Mount Holly also pay that city’s rate, and homes outside city limits may pay a small fire district tax.

In South Carolina, an owner-occupied primary residence is assessed at 4 percent of value instead of 6 percent, once you apply with the county. That is one reason York County payments can look different at the same price.

Straight answers

Mortgage calculator questions

What does this mortgage calculator include?

Principal and interest, based on the price, down payment, rate and term you enter, plus property taxes and homeowners insurance if you add them. It does not include mortgage insurance, HOA dues, flood insurance or closing costs.

What is PMI, and when do I pay it?

Private mortgage insurance protects the lender, not you. On a conventional loan it is usually required when you put down less than 20 percent. FHA loans have their own mortgage insurance premiums. Your lender will show the cost on your Loan Estimate.

Should I choose a 15-, 20- or 30-year term?

A shorter term usually means a higher monthly payment but far less interest over the life of the loan. A 30-year term keeps the payment lower and leaves room in your budget. Try each term above to see the difference, then talk it through with a lender.

How do I estimate property taxes in Gaston County?

Multiply the assessed value by the rate per $100. Gaston County’s rate is 59.9 cents per $100 as of July 1, 2026, so a home assessed at $400,000 would owe about $2,396 a year in county tax, before any city or fire district tax. Check the county’s tax rate chart for the city rates.

Do HOA dues affect how much I can borrow?

Yes. Lenders count HOA dues in your monthly obligations, so a home with higher dues can lower the price you qualify for. Ask for the dues and any special assessments early.
