A Saturday in Belmont

Coffee and the History Walk downtown, an afternoon on the Catawba, and an evening in the garden.

By Ally Bohanan · Reviewed November 19, 2026

Belmont

Belmont rewards a slow Saturday. Everything in this plan is within a short drive of Ally’s office on North Main Street, and the morning half needs no car at all.

Morning: Main Street on foot

Park once and walk. Belmont’s downtown spans three historic districts, and the town’s self-guided History Walk introduces the people and buildings behind them. Locally owned shops, coffee houses and Belmont Bookshop are all within a few blocks, according to Go Gaston.

Late morning: Stowe Park

The green at the center of downtown hosts farmers markets, concerts and Movies in the Park in warmer months. In December it fills with decorated trees for the Festival of Trees.

Afternoon: the river

Choose your pace. Kevin Loftin Riverfront Park sits on the Catawba River. South Point Access has a sandy Lake Wylie shoreline. Rocky Branch Park is the town’s mountain-bike trail system.

Evening: Daniel Stowe Conservancy

End the day in the gardens south of town. From late November through December 30, Holidays at the Garden lights the grounds on select nights from 5 to 10 p.m. Buy tickets ahead.

If you are thinking about living here

A Saturday like this is the easiest way to feel whether Belmont fits. Read the Belmont community guide, or compare Belmont with Mount Holly, its neighbor upriver.

Checked against: Go Gaston: Belmont · Downtown Belmont · Daniel Stowe Conservancy. Details change, so confirm hours, dates and prices with each source before you go.
