A Weekend Exploring Gaston County

A summit hike, a planetarium, a nutcracker museum and two Main Streets, in one easy weekend.

By Ally Bohanan
Reviewed November 19, 2026

Stops
5
Towns
Gastonia, Kings Mountain, Mount Holly
When
Weekend · Saturday–Sunday
Read
2 min

The short answer

On Saturday, hike Crowders Mountain in the morning, spend the afternoon at the Schiele Museum and its planetarium in Gastonia, and have dinner downtown. On Sunday, have coffee and see Nutty for Nutcrackers in Kings Mountain, then finish on Main Street and the RiverHawk Greenway in Mount Holly.

Updated November 19, 2026

Gaston County is easy to underestimate from I-85. Give it a weekend and it becomes a place with mountains, a planetarium and the largest downtown in the county.

The Crowders Mountain Trail at 522 Park Office Lane is 2.5 miles one way over natural surface, boardwalk and gravel. The Backside Trail is a steeper half-mile for the final climb (Go Gaston).

If you live here

This weekend covers three different Gaston County towns. Gastonia is the county’s largest downtown, on I-85 and US-321. Kings Mountain sits at the western edge of the county near the state line. Mount Holly is on the Catawba River just across from Charlotte. Read the Gastonia, Kings Mountain and Mount Holly guides. Straight answers

Before you go

What are Crowders Mountain State Park’s hours?

Gates open at 8 a.m. year-round. They close at 6 p.m. from November through February, at 8 p.m. in March, April and October, and at 9 p.m. from May through September. The visitor center is open daily from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and the park is closed on Christmas Day. Gates are locked at the posted time, so plan to be back at your car (NC State Parks).

Where do I park for the Crowders Mountain Trail?

Use the Sparrow Springs Access and Visitor Center at 522 Park Office Lane, Kings Mountain. The park also has the Linwood Road Access at 4611 Linwood Road, Gastonia, and the Boulders Access at 108 Vandyke Road, Kings Mountain (NC State Parks).

When is the Schiele Museum open?

Monday to Friday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Sunday 1 to 5 p.m. It closes on Easter, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day (Schiele Museum). Check the museum’s site for admission and planetarium show times.

When is Nutty for Nutcrackers open?

Thursday and Friday 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Saturday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., at 210 S. Piedmont Ave. in Kings Mountain (Nutty for Nutcrackers). It is closed on the Sunday of this plan, so swap it to Saturday if the nutcrackers are a must.
