About · Our team

Jessi Sanford, Realtor.

Jessi lives in Stanley, North Carolina, with her husband and their two sons, and works with buyers and sellers throughout Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly and nearby communities.

Call Jessi Email Jessi

The short answer

Jessi Sanford is a North Carolina Realtor with Ally Bohanan Real Estate, brokered by Northstar Real Estate, LLC. She lives in Stanley with her family and helps buyers and sellers in Gastonia, Belmont, Mount Holly and nearby Gaston County towns, with a particular focus on first-time buyers and clear, hands-on communication from the first showing to closing day.

Updated November 2026

Who is Jessi Sanford?

Jessi brings local insight and a real passion for helping families, individuals and first-time buyers feel confident at every step. Her roots in Stanley drive her commitment to clients across Gaston County, and she treats every client like family, with honesty, respect and dependable support.

“I take a true hands-on approach from start to finish because I believe real estate should feel personal, organized, and stress-free.”

How does Jessi work with clients?

  • Personal support for buyers and sellers in Gastonia and nearby communities
  • Clear, consistent communication from start to finish
  • Hands-on guidance from the first showing to closing day
  • Community-focused service rooted in honesty and long-term relationships

From scheduling thoughtful showings to helping sellers prepare their homes, her goal is to make the process simpler and guide you to the closing table, backed by Ally Bohanan and the rest of the team.

    First-time buyers

    Buying your first home with Jessi

    1. Talk with a lender

      Learn what you can comfortably spend. Ally’s preferred lender is a good place to start, and the mortgage calculator helps you test rough numbers.

    2. Pick the towns

      Jessi can tell you how Stanley, Gastonia, Belmont and Mount Holly differ day to day. Find My Carolina Community helps you narrow the list.

    3. Tour and compare

      Jessi schedules showings around your week and helps you compare each home on condition, cost and fit.

    4. Offer, due diligence, closing

      She explains every term in plain English, keeps the deadlines on track and stays with you through the keys.

    Selling

    How does Jessi help sellers?

    Jessi helps sellers get their homes ready for the market and keeps showings organized and on schedule, with clear updates after each tour. Pricing starts with Ally’s in-person pricing consultation, and the selling timeline turns the preparation into a week-by-week plan.

    Because she works inside Ally’s team, you get one point of contact who knows your file, with an experienced broker and a builder’s perspective behind her.
