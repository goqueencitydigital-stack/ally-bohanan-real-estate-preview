NC First-Time Home Buyer Assistance Programs 2026

By Ally BohananPublished May 9, 2026Updated November 16, 20265 min read

The short answer

NC first-time home buyer programs offer meaningful financial assistance to eligible buyers in Gastonia, Gaston County, and across North Carolina, and most buyers have no idea how much help is available.

NC first-time home buyer programs offer meaningful financial assistance to eligible buyers in Gastonia, Gaston County, and across North Carolina, and most buyers have no idea how much help is available. Down payment assistance, below-market interest rates, and forgivable loans can be combined in ways that dramatically reduce the upfront cost of buying your first home. If you have been sitting on the sidelines waiting to save more, understanding what programs exist may change your timeline significantly.

This guide covers the primary assistance programs available to first-time buyers in North Carolina in 2026, how to qualify, and how working with the right lender and agent makes the difference between getting the assistance you deserve and leaving it on the table.

NC Home Advantage Mortgage Program

The NC Home Advantage Mortgage is the flagship program from the NC Housing Finance Agency (NCHFA). It provides first-time buyers and qualified move-up buyers with a competitive fixed interest rate mortgage paired with up to 3% to 5% of the loan amount in down payment assistance.

Who Qualifies

  • First-time buyers (not owned a home in the past three years) or qualified military veterans
  • Annual income must fall within NCHFA limits, which vary by county and household size (Gaston County limits for 2026 are updated on the NCHFA website)
  • Purchase price must be within program limits
  • Must occupy the home as a primary residence
  • Must complete an approved homebuyer education course

Key Benefits

  • Below-market fixed interest rates that reduce your monthly payment
  • Down payment assistance of up to 5% of the loan amount, provided as a second mortgage that is forgiven over 15 years at 20% per year if you stay in the home
  • Available with FHA, VA, USDA, and conventional loan types

For a Gaston County buyer purchasing a $290,000 home with 5% down payment assistance, this program can contribute up to $14,500 toward your purchase, potentially eliminating or dramatically reducing your need for personal savings at closing. Visit our preferred lender page to connect with lenders who are certified NC Home Advantage program participants.

NC 1st Home Advantage Down Payment

The NC 1st Home Advantage Down Payment provides an additional $15,000 in down payment assistance specifically for first-time buyers and military veterans. This is structured as a deferred second mortgage with zero percent interest that is forgiven at 20% per year beginning in year 11 of ownership.

This program is layered on top of the NC Home Advantage Mortgage and can be combined for total assistance that covers a substantial portion of both down payment and closing costs for many Gastonia-area buyers. The combination of programs has helped buyers purchase in the $250,000 to $350,000 range with very limited personal cash at closing.

Community Partners Loan Pool (CPLP)

The Community Partners Loan Pool provides down payment and closing cost assistance up to $50,000 for eligible buyers in specific targeted areas and working with participating Community Home Buying Organizations. Buyers in qualifying Gastonia census tracts or working through eligible nonprofits can access this larger assistance amount, which makes it one of the most generous programs available.

Income limits and eligibility requirements are stricter for CPLP than for the standard NC Home Advantage programs, and buyers must purchase within designated areas. Talk to a NCHFA-approved lender to determine whether your specific situation qualifies.

Federal Programs Available to NC Buyers

FHA Loans

FHA loans require a minimum 3.5% down payment and are widely used by first-time buyers in Gastonia. Credit score requirements are lower than conventional loans, and FHA’s underwriting guidelines are more flexible for buyers with limited credit history. The downside is that FHA requires both upfront and annual mortgage insurance premiums that add to the total loan cost.

USDA Rural Development Loans

Many areas of Gaston County, particularly rural communities like Dallas, Cherryville, and parts of the county outside the Gastonia city limits, may qualify for USDA Section 502 direct or guaranteed loans. These programs offer zero down payment for income-eligible buyers in qualifying rural areas. The eligibility map for Gaston County is worth checking if you are considering a more rural property, as the qualifying zone boundaries may surprise you.

VA Loans

Veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses may use VA home loans with zero down payment, no private mortgage insurance, and competitive interest rates. VA loans are consistently one of the strongest mortgage products available, and Gaston County’s veteran population makes this a common and valuable resource. There is no income limit for VA loans.

Gaston County-Specific Resources

In addition to statewide programs, Gaston County periodically offers local homebuyer assistance through community development block grant funding and partnerships with nonprofit housing organizations. These programs are not always consistently funded and may have waiting lists, but they can provide additional assistance for income-qualifying buyers.

The Gaston Chamber of Commerce and Gaston County’s Community Development office can provide current information on locally available programs that supplement state assistance.

Steps to Accessing NC First-Time Buyer Programs

  1. Get pre-approved through an NCHFA-participating lender. Not all lenders offer the NC Home Advantage Mortgage. Your agent can connect you with lenders who have completed the required training.
  2. Complete a homebuyer education course. Most NC first-time buyer programs require an approved education course (HUD-approved online options are available). The investment of a few hours pays off in thousands of dollars of assistance access.
  3. Confirm your income and purchase price eligibility. NCHFA updates income and purchase price limits periodically. Your lender will confirm current limits for Gaston County.
  4. Work with an experienced local agent. An agent who understands assistance programs helps you structure your offer and timeline to maximize what you receive.

Common Questions About NC First-Time Buyer Programs

Buyers often have misconceptions about who qualifies and how these programs work:

  • “I make too much to qualify.” Income limits are higher than many buyers assume. Households earning $75,000 to $95,000 or more may still qualify depending on county and household size.
  • “My credit is not good enough.” FHA allows credit scores as low as 580 for the standard 3.5% down program. Some programs have even more flexible credit requirements.
  • “I owned a home years ago so I do not qualify.” Most programs define first-time buyer as not having owned a primary residence in the past three years, not as someone who has never owned.
  • “These programs make your offer less competitive.” In most standard transactions, seller awareness of buyer assistance programs does not affect offer competitiveness. Your offer looks the same to a seller whether you use assistance or not.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

What first-time home buyer programs are available in NC?

North Carolina’s primary programs include the NC Home Advantage Mortgage with down payment assistance, the NC 1st Home Advantage Down Payment program providing up to $15,000, the Community Partners Loan Pool for eligible buyers in targeted areas, and federal programs including FHA, USDA, and VA loans.

How much down payment assistance is available for first-time buyers in NC?

Through combined NC Housing Finance Agency programs, eligible first-time buyers can receive up to 5% of the loan amount plus $15,000 in additional down payment assistance. Community Partners Loan Pool can provide up to $50,000 for qualifying buyers in designated areas.

Do I have to be a first-time buyer to qualify for NC assistance programs?

Most NC Housing Finance Agency programs define first-time buyer as not having owned a primary residence in the previous three years. Military veterans are often exempt from this requirement entirely. If you owned a home several years ago but do not currently own, you may still qualify.

Can I use NC first-time buyer programs to purchase in Gastonia?

Yes. Gaston County and the Gastonia area are fully eligible for NC Home Advantage Mortgage, down payment assistance, and other state programs. Income and purchase price limits apply and are updated periodically by NCHFA.

What is the income limit for NC first-time buyer programs in Gaston County?

Income limits for NCHFA programs vary by household size and are updated periodically. For current Gaston County limits, check the NC Housing Finance Agency website or consult with an NCHFA-participating lender. Limits are generally more generous than buyers assume.
