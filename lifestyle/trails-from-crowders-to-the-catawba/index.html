Trails From Crowders Mountain to the Catawba

Seven trail systems within reach of Ally’s office, from a summit climb to a 2,100-acre greenway.

By Ally Bohanan · Reviewed November 19, 2026

Gaston & York counties

Trail access is one of the first things relocating buyers ask Ally about. These are the systems worth knowing, with the distances their managers publish.

Crowders Mountain State Park

The Crowders Mountain Trail runs 2.5 miles one way, and the Backside Trail is a steep half-mile to the top (Go Gaston).

Gateway Trail, Kings Mountain

A 4.8-mile trail on the Kings Mountain side of the range (Go Gaston).

George Poston Park, Lowell

About 12 miles of trails near the South Fork River (Go Gaston).

RiverHawk Greenway, Mount Holly

A paved, accessible greenway along the Catawba River and part of the Carolina Thread Trail. It starts at Tuckaseegee Park.

Rocky Branch Park, Belmont

Belmont’s mountain-bike trails.

Anne Springs Close Greenway, Fort Mill

2,100 acres with more than 40 miles of trails, at 2573 Lake Haigler Dr. Entry is by day pass or membership (ASC Greenway).

U.S. National Whitewater Center, Charlotte

More than 1,300 acres and 40–50 miles of trails on the Catawba (Whitewater Center).

Checked against: Go Gaston · Anne Springs Close Greenway · U.S. National Whitewater Center. Details change, so confirm hours, dates and prices with each source before you go.
