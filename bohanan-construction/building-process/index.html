Bohanan Construction · The process

From first conversation to front door.

Five steps that organize the hundreds of decisions in a custom home, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

Start Your Build I already own a lot

The short answer

Bohanan Construction builds in five steps: a first conversation about how you live, a look at your land, design and selections with Ally, a personalized proposal and agreement, then construction overseen on site by Chantzler through the final walkthrough. Timing depends on your project and is discussed honestly up front.

Updated November 2026
Own a lot already? Start with the site evaluation checklist. Still looking? See finding land.

Five steps

How a Bohanan build moves.

  1. Dream and discover

    An unhurried first conversation with Chantzler and Ally about who the home is for, what matters most, and your timing. You also get an honest look at budget ranges and next steps.

  2. Land and location

    Whether you own land, are under contract or are still looking, the team helps you understand what a homesite needs before the design is final: access, slope, clearing, drainage, utilities, HOA guidelines, setbacks and permitting.

  3. Design and selections

    Choose a plan to customize or bring your own, then shape the exterior and interior with Ally’s design guidance: exterior materials, roofline, windows and doors, flooring, cabinetry, countertops, tile and lighting.

  4. Pricing and planning

    Your answers become a personalized proposal built from your land, your plan and your selections, with scope, allowances and assumptions spelled out. A construction agreement follows when you are ready.

  5. Build and welcome home

    Permitting and site preparation, regular progress updates, selections installed as planned, and a final walkthrough before move-in. Chantzler oversees construction on site the whole way.

About timelines and pricing

Every custom home is different. Design, permitting, site conditions, selections and weather all affect how long a build takes, so the team discusses realistic expectations for your project instead of quoting a one-size-fits-all schedule.

Pricing works the same way. Foundation type, utilities, exterior materials and finish level all influence cost, and proposals explain scope, allowances, assumptions and exclusions so you can compare decisions with confidence. Coffered ceilings, herringbone floors, stained beams and thoughtful trim are planned early, so they are part of the budget rather than an afterthought.

Straight answers

Process questions

How does building a custom home with Bohanan Construction work?

It starts with a conversation about your goals, land, budget and timing. The team then helps you understand your homesite, shape the plan and selections, and prepares a personalized proposal before a construction agreement.

How are construction estimates developed?

Each estimate is built from your project: the land and site conditions, the plan and its size, and your exterior and interior selections. Proposals explain scope, allowances, assumptions and exclusions.

How do finish selections affect the budget?

Finishes are one of the biggest variables in a custom home. Cabinetry, countertops, flooring, tile and exterior materials can change the cost of two homes with the same floor plan. Making decisions early helps the proposal stay accurate.

How does financing work for a new home?

Most clients use construction or construction-to-permanent financing from a lender. Bohanan Construction is not a lender, but the team can talk you through the process. Ally’s preferred lender is a good place to start.

How long will my build take?

Every custom home is different. Design, permitting, site conditions, selections and weather all affect timing, so the team discusses realistic expectations for your specific project.
