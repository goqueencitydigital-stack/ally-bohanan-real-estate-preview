Overview

Stunning 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom home in Summerhouse at Paddlers Cove. The home is arguably located in the best location. On a cul-de-sac street, only 1 neighbor, and walking distance to the newly upcoming Harris Teeter/ Shopping center! GREAT location while still enjoying privacy. The moment you enter the home you will feel how well its been taken care of. Wood floors, huge kitchen island (can fit 5-6 barstools!), walk-in pantry, stunning custom fireplace/ shelfing. Garage has epoxy floors PLUS and mini-split...making this a perfect space to fit any of your needs. Primary bedroom faces the beautiful manicured backyard. Primary bath offers 2 separate vanities, shower, and a huge walk-in closet. Backyard offers a large screened in porch, patio area, and a fenced in backyard. Location...location...location...& do not miss the neighborhood pool! Welcome HOME!
