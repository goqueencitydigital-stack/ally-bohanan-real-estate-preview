A Saturday in Belmont

Coffee and the History Walk downtown, an afternoon on the Catawba, and an evening in the garden.

By Ally Bohanan
Reviewed November 19, 2026

Stops
4
Towns
Belmont, Lake Wylie
When
One day · Morning to evening
Read
1 min

The short answer

Spend the morning on foot in downtown Belmont: coffee, Belmont Bookshop, the History Walk and Stowe Park. Spend the afternoon on the water at Kevin Loftin Riverfront Park or South Point Access on Lake Wylie, then end the day at Daniel Stowe Conservancy south of town.

Updated November 19, 2026

Belmont rewards a slow Saturday. Everything in this plan is within a short drive of Ally’s office on North Main Street, and the morning half needs no car at all.

The plan

01

Stop 01Belmont

Morning: Main Street on foot

Park once and walk. Belmont’s downtown spans three historic districts, and the town’s self-guided History Walk introduces the people and buildings behind them. Locally owned shops, coffee houses and Belmont Bookshop are all within a few blocks, according to Go Gaston.

Late morning: Stowe Park

The green at the center of downtown hosts farmers markets, concerts and Movies in the Park in warmer months. In December it fills with decorated trees for the Festival of Trees.

Afternoon: the river

Choose your pace. Kevin Loftin Riverfront Park sits on the Catawba River. South Point Access has a sandy Lake Wylie shoreline. Rocky Branch Park is the town’s mountain-bike trail system.

Evening: Daniel Stowe Conservancy

End the day in the gardens south of town. From late November through December 30, Holidays at the Garden lights the grounds on select nights from 5 to 10 p.m. Buy tickets ahead.

If you live here

For people who live in Belmont, this is a normal Saturday. Downtown is walkable, the Catawba River is at the edge of town, and Lake Wylie’s public shoreline is a short drive south. Belmont sits just over the river from Charlotte, off I-85 on the Gaston County side, according to Go Gaston. Read the Belmont community guide or compare Belmont with Mount Holly. Straight answers

Before you go

Is there a fee at South Point Access?

Yes. Gaston County lists South Point Access, 175 Boat Launch Road, at $7 per vehicle and $20 per bus. Winter hours, November 1 through March 31, are 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. The swim beach opens only from Memorial Day through Labor Day (Gaston County).

How much does Daniel Stowe Conservancy cost?

Daytime admission to The Gardens at Stowe is $18.95 plus tax for adults, $16.95 for seniors 60 and up and $10.95 for children 2 to 12. Children under 2 and members get in free, and day passes are sold in person. The trails, dog park, Trailhead Store and Farmhouse Garden Center are free (Daniel Stowe Conservancy). Holidays at the Garden is a separate ticket. Entry runs from 5 to 9 p.m., and parking is free (Holidays at the Garden).

Can I bring my dog?

At South Point Access, dogs must stay on a leash six feet or shorter and are not allowed in the beach area (Gaston County). Daniel Stowe Conservancy has a free dog park and free scenic trails. Check its site for the rules inside The Gardens (Daniel Stowe Conservancy).

Do I need a car for this day?

Not for the morning. Downtown Belmont is compact enough to park once and walk to coffee, the bookshop, the History Walk and Stowe Park. You will want a car for the afternoon and evening, because the riverfront, South Point and the gardens are outside downtown.
