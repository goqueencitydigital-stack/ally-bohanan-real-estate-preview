Meet Ally

A Realtor who knows how houses are built.

Ally Bohanan is a North Carolina and South Carolina broker-in-charge working from an office on Main Street in Belmont. She is also the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction, the custom-home company she runs with Chantzler Bohanan.

That means when you walk a house with Ally, she notices the details. Is that wall original? What would moving that kitchen involve? What should the inspection look at? Clients say it is the difference they feel most.
