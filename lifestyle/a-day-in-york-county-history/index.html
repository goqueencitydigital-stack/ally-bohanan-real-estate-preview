A Day in York County History

Nearly 200 registered sites in York, an 800-acre living-history farm and Rock Hill’s Mural Mile.

By Ally Bohanan
Reviewed November 19, 2026

York County, SC

York County, South Carolina, keeps its history close to the surface. This day moves from a living-history farm to a historic downtown, and ends with murals in Old Town Rock Hill.

Morning: Historic Brattonsville

An 800-acre living-history farm outside York (Visit York County).

Midday: York, the White Rose City

Nearly 200 historically registered locations. Walk Congress Street, browse Books-4-Ewe, and stop at Yorkville Marketplace.

Afternoon: Rock Hill’s Old Town

The Mural Mile, the Freedom Walkway, the Center for the Arts and Glencairn Garden (Visit York County).

Detour: Fort Mill

The Fort Mill History Museum on Main Street (Visit York County).

Curious what living in one of these towns is like? Read the York, Rock Hill and Fort Mill guides.

Checked against: Visit York County. Details change, so confirm hours, dates and prices with each source before you go.
