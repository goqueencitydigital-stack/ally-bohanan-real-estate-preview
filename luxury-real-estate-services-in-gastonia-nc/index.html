The short answer

Luxury real estate services in Gastonia and the Charlotte area combine careful pricing with presentation and privacy. Updated November 2026

What changes at the top

How is selling a luxury home different?

  • Pricing needs more judgment. Fewer comparable sales, and the features that matter most, such as frontage, acreage and custom work, rarely match.
  • Presentation carries more weight. Buyers at higher prices compare homes across the region, often online first.
  • Privacy matters. Showings by appointment, buyers qualified before they tour, and care with what the photos reveal.
  • Appraisals can be harder. A thin set of comparable sales means Ally prepares notes on upgrades and construction for the appraiser.
  • Timelines can run longer. The buyer pool is smaller, so pricing and preparation have to be right from the first week.

The luxury plan

How does Ally market a luxury listing?

  1. Pricing consultation

    A walk-through with notes on construction, finishes and setting, then a price range grounded in sales and replacement cost.

  2. Design and staging

    Ally, who leads design at Bohanan Construction, edits rooms so the architecture leads. See home staging.

  3. Editorial photography

    Light, angles and details that show craftsmanship, and video where it suits the home.

  4. A story buyers can trust

    The listing explains what was built, upgraded or restored, in plain language a buyer’s inspector would agree with.

  5. Private, qualified showings

    By appointment, with buyers who are pre-approved or have shown proof of funds.

  6. Negotiation and closing

    Every term weighed, and the appraisal and due diligence managed closely.

Straight answers

Luxury seller questions

How do you price a luxury home with few comparable sales?

Ally starts with the closest recent sales, adjusts for frontage, acreage, construction quality and finish, and studies the competing homes a buyer at that price is touring. Because she works with a builder, she can also estimate what it would cost to build something similar today, which is often how luxury buyers think.

Can my listing be kept private?

There are options for limiting exposure, and each one has trade-offs. A quieter launch protects privacy but reaches fewer buyers. Ally will explain what is allowed under MLS and brokerage rules and what fits your situation.

Should I stage a luxury home?

Usually, yes. Buyers at higher prices expect a home to photograph and show beautifully. Ally’s design background helps her edit rooms so the architecture and finishes lead. See home staging.

Do you also represent luxury buyers?

Yes. Ally represents buyers of luxury and custom homes, including clients relocating from out of state. See buying a luxury home.

Can I sell my current home while building a custom home?

Yes. Ally can time the sale around the construction schedule with Bohanan Construction. Amy Gordon’s review describes exactly that.
