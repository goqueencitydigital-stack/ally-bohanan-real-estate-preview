Clover SC Homebuyer Guide: Schools, Prices & Commute

How Clover compares with Fort Mill and Lake Wylie, how to research Clover School District, what drives prices, and how the commutes work.

By Ally BohananPublished May 5, 2026Updated November 16, 20263 min read

The short answer

Clover is a small town in western York County, South Carolina, served by Clover School District, which also covers the Lake Wylie area. Buyers choose it for small-town character, more land and generally lower prices than Fort Mill. The trade-off is a longer drive to Charlotte, though Gastonia is close via US-321.

Key takeaways

  1. Clover School District (York School District 2) covers Clover and much of the Lake Wylie area; confirm the assigned schools for each address.
  2. Homes generally cost less than comparable homes in Fort Mill, with more older homes, acreage and rural parcels.
  3. Gastonia and Gaston County are a short drive north on US-321; Charlotte takes longer.
  4. Housing ranges from historic homes near Main Street to newer subdivisions toward Lake Wylie and rural land.
  5. South Carolina's 4% assessment ratio for owner-occupied homes helps keep property tax bills down.

Clover sits in western York County, just south of the North Carolina line and west of Lake Wylie. It has kept its small-town feel even as growth has spread across the county from Fort Mill and Lake Wylie. For buyers who want a South Carolina address, a well-regarded school district and more space for the money, it's worth a serious look.

Why are more buyers looking at Clover?

  • Value: homes in Clover generally cost less than similar homes in Fort Mill, and land is easier to find.
  • Schools: Clover School District is a big draw for families. More on researching it below.
  • Character: a historic downtown, local businesses and community events like the Clover Feis Scottish festival.
  • Location: close to Gastonia and Gaston County jobs, with Lake Wylie a few minutes east.
  • Taxes: South Carolina assesses an owner-occupied legal residence at 4% of value, compared with 6% for other property. You apply with York County to get the lower rate.

Our post on why more buyers are moving to Clover covers the lifestyle side in more depth.

What do homes cost in Clover?

Prices change with the market, so check current listings and recent sales rather than a fixed range. In general:

Well-priced, move-in-ready homes in the most popular price bands can go quickly, so get pre-approved before you start touring. See current homes and town details on our Clover community page.

How should you research Clover schools?

Clover School District, officially York School District 2, serves Clover and much of the Lake Wylie area of York County, with one main high school, Clover High School. To research it:

  • Start with the district's own site at Clover School District for schools, programs and attendance zones.
  • Review each school's ratings and data on the South Carolina School Report Cards site.
  • Confirm the assigned schools for any specific address with the district. Zones can change as the district grows.
  • Visit schools and talk with staff about programs that matter to you, such as AP, dual enrollment, arts or athletics.

Fort Mill is served by a different district, Fort Mill School District (York 4), and Rock Hill by Rock Hill Schools, so school assignment can change from one side of a road to the other near district lines.

How long is the commute from Clover?

Drive times depend on where in the Clover area you live and when you travel, so test your route at rush hour.

  • Gastonia: a short drive north on US-321, one of Clover's biggest advantages for Gaston County workers.
  • Charlotte: via US-321 to I-85, or east through Lake Wylie toward SC-49 and I-485. Expect a longer drive than from Fort Mill or Lake Wylie.
  • Rock Hill and York: reachable on state roads through western York County.
  • Charlotte Douglas Airport: via I-85 or I-485, depending on your starting point.

Which parts of Clover fit which buyers?

Historic downtown Clover

Older homes on generous lots near Main Street, within walking distance of local businesses. Expect to budget for updates and inspect carefully for roof, HVAC, plumbing and crawlspace condition.

Lake Wylie side

Communities east of town toward Lake Wylie give you access to the lake lifestyle, sometimes with community water access, often for less than prime Lake Wylie and Tega Cay locations. If you're thinking about the water, read buying waterfront property in Lake Wylie.

Newer subdivisions

Newer construction with HOA amenities in the Clover School District. Review HOA documents and builder warranties before you commit.

Rural and acreage

Land, farms and room to build west and south of town. Check well and septic, the survey and any easements.

Frequently asked questions

Is Clover SC a good place to live?

For buyers who want small-town character, more space and a South Carolina address within reach of Gastonia and Charlotte, many find it a strong fit. The trade-offs are a longer Charlotte commute and fewer in-town amenities than Fort Mill.

Is Lake Wylie in the Clover School District?

Much of the Lake Wylie area of York County is served by Clover School District. Confirm assignment for the specific address with the district.

How do I get the 4% property tax rate in South Carolina?

You apply for the legal residence (owner-occupied) assessment with the York County assessor after you buy. Ask your closing attorney about timing.

Can I find land to build on near Clover?

Yes, land and acreage are more common around Clover than in Fort Mill or Lake Wylie. Check zoning, utilities, perc tests and the survey before you buy.
