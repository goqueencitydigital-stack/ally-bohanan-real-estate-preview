Gaston County, North Carolina

Mount Holly, from Main Street to the river

Mount Holly is a river town with a short, busy Main Street. The best way to understand it is the route most locals take: coffee and a bookstore downtown, then out to the parks and greenway along the Catawba.

Mount Holly

The short answer

Mount Holly is a river town with a short, busy Main Street. A Main Street that leads to the river and the RiverHawk Greenway. Ally Bohanan has 3 recorded sales in Mount Holly and 1 home for sale there now (Zillow, November 19, 2026).

Updated November 2026

Key facts

  • Main StreetCoffee, books, wine and bourbon
  • GreenwayRiverHawk Greenway, part of the Carolina Thread Trail
  • PaddlingDutchman’s Creek launch at River Street Park
  • Lake accessTailrace Marina on Lake Wylie

The local route

Main Street to the water, in order

Downtown first, then the parks along the Catawba.

  1. 104 N. Main St.

    Cleary’s Bookstore & Black Sheep Coffee

    A woman-owned independent bookstore that shares its space with a coffee bar.

  2. 101 W. Central Ave.

    Owl Legs Coffee

    Veteran-owned and inspired by Berlin’s coffee culture.

  3. 100 N. Main St.

    The Holland

    A wine bar and restaurant downtown. Local Logic, at 128 S. Main, pours cocktails and bourbon.

  4. Mount Holly

    Tuckaseegee Park

    Playgrounds, sports fields, a dog park, a pollinator garden and a trailhead for the RiverHawk Greenway.

  5. Mount Holly

    River Street Park

    Walking trails, fishing, a nine-hole disc golf course and a natural canoe and kayak launch onto Dutchman’s Creek.

  6. Mount Holly

    Tailrace Marina

    Boating and paddling access to Lake Wylie.
