The short answer

A luxury custom home in Gastonia, NC is built from your plans on land you choose, rather than bought finished. Ally Bohanan helps you find and evaluate the land as a licensed broker, then Bohanan Construction, the Gastonia builder she manages with owner Chantzler, designs and builds the home with you.

Updated November 2026

Custom homes in Gaston County, designed around you

For buyers who want a specific layout, more land or a higher level of finish than resale listings offer, building can be the better path. Most clients start at one of three places: they already own land, they have a plan in mind but no property yet, or they are still comparing building with buying.

Why work with a Realtor who builds

Land, design and the build, read together.

Most agents can show you land. It helps when the person showing it also works inside a construction company. Ally can look at a lot with questions about access, utilities, slope, drainage and restrictions in mind, and bring them to Chantzler before you make an offer.

On the design side, her background in real estate and home staging shapes how each home looks, lives and holds its value. You get one point of contact who speaks both languages, from the first showing to the final walkthrough.

The custom home process

From consultation to move-in.

The full process

  1. Dream and discover

    An unhurried first conversation with Chantzler and Ally about who the home is for, what matters most, and your timing. You also get an honest look at budget ranges and next steps.

  2. Land and location

    Whether you own land, are under contract or are still looking, the team helps you understand what a homesite needs before the design is final: access, slope, clearing, drainage, utilities, HOA guidelines, setbacks and permitting.

  3. Design and selections

    Choose a plan to customize or bring your own, then shape the exterior and interior with Ally’s design guidance: exterior materials, roofline, windows and doors, flooring, cabinetry, countertops, tile and lighting.

  4. Pricing and planning

    Your answers become a personalized proposal built from your land, your plan and your selections, with scope, allowances and assumptions spelled out. A construction agreement follows when you are ready.

  5. Build and welcome home

    Permitting and site preparation, regular progress updates, selections installed as planned, and a final walkthrough before move-in. Chantzler oversees construction on site the whole way.

Straight answers

Custom home questions

Who builds luxury custom homes in Gastonia, NC?

Bohanan Construction, a Gastonia builder led by Chantzler and Ally Bohanan, builds custom homes, new construction and renovations in Gaston County. See Bohanan Construction.

Should I buy a home or build one in Gaston County?

It depends on your land, your timing, the location you want and how involved you want to be. Should I Buy or Build? gives you a side-by-side brief based on your answers.

Can one person help with the land and the house?

Ally Bohanan is a licensed real estate broker with Ally Bohanan Real Estate and the business manager and designer at Bohanan Construction. She can help you find and evaluate land, then guide the design and selections for the home you build on it. Real estate services are offered separately from construction.

What makes a custom home feel luxurious?

Usually the details you live with every day: a plaster range hood, coffered ceilings, arched French doors, a built-in buffet, a covered porch with a stained wood ceiling. Explore a Bohanan home to see them up close.
